# Strategy

One layer of policy: a name unique within its pipeline, a kind, a summary, validation problems, an attach hook, and an execute function. Built-in strategies take an options record with defaults; custom strategies are built with `Strategy.custom`. See [[src/PuduLangResilience/Strategy]].
