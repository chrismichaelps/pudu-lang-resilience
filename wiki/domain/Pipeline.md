# Pipeline

Strategies composed outermost first around a callback, validated as a whole when built, reporting each execution's start and end. A pipeline may be nested inside another as one strategy, and named pipelines are shared through the registry. See [[src/PuduLangResilience/Pipeline]] and [[src/PuduLangResilience/Registry]].
